To track visits across all offices (Toronto, Berlin, Stockholm, Sydney), connect this site to a free Google Analytics 4 property. Takes about 5 minutes.

2
Copy your Measurement ID — it looks like G-ABC123XYZ
3

What's being tracked
These GA4 events fire automatically once you replace G-XXXXXXXXXX with a real Measurement ID.
Event name When it fires Useful for
page_view Every page load on the site Visits per page, day/week/month trends, unique visitors
science_card_view When a science card modal is opened Most-viewed science mechanisms; card popularity ranking
source_link_click When a source/DOI link is clicked (card or modal) Engagement with academic sources; which cards generate research interest
filter_applied When a filter chip is toggled on the Science page Which filters (opinions, tools, use cases) are most used
