HOW GREAT LEADERS ACT

Our science-informed point of view on leadership.

1

Clarify Intent

Without a clear goal, thinking lacks direction. Great leaders define what success looks like before anything starts.

2

Ask Better Questions

When leaders give answers first, people often process information passively. Great leaders use questions to activate attention, reflection, participation, and deeper thinking.

3

Activate Participation

When people only listen passively, thinking, learning and engagement stays low. Great leaders create moments where people are switched on.

4

Structure How People Think and Decide Together

Unstructured discussions lead to unclear outcomes. Great leaders unleash the group's full potential by creating safe and clear structure for contribution and decision making.

5

Make People Understand Deeply

Sharing information isn't enough. Great leaders educate by helping people reflect, process and apply what matters.

6

Create Ownership & Commitment

When people are excluded, they disengage from change. Great leaders involve people in shaping direction, decisions, and outcomes.

7

Reinforce Over Time

One-off efforts don't change performance. Great leaders create simple, repeatable ways to reinforce learning, action, and behavior over time.
